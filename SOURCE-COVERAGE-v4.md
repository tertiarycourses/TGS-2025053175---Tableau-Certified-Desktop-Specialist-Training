## Preservation decisions

The legacy deck's useful concepts were retained and reorganised around the current official objective list. Repetitive click-by-click slide runs were not copied into the new deck; their substance appears as detailed Learner Guide and lab procedures. This honours the course rule that slides teach concepts while procedures live in the guide.

## Deliberate de-emphasis

Legacy pages centred on specialised Server/Cloud administration, Data Catalog, Virtual Connections, obsolete `.tde` behaviour and long product-version troubleshooting sequences are retained only as adjacent context where useful. They are not given equal teaching time because they are outside the official Desktop Specialist objectives used for this course build.
